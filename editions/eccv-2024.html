Workshop contributions

Accepted Papers

Twelve papers spanning eye tracking, event-based sensing, visual-inertial SLAM, efficient transformers, pose estimation and egocentric action recognition.

Vigars; Louis Berridge; Owen Morgan; Kemi Jacobs; Ben Menzies; Gabriel Homewood; Christopher J. Mair; Taru Muhonen; Rory Clark; Paolo Baesso; Dave Trickett; Iain Wallace

  3. Ultra-Efficient On-Device Object Detection on AI-Integrated Smart Glasses With TinyissimoYOLOJulian Moosmann; Pietro Bonazzi; Philipp Mayer; Yawei Li; Sizhen Bian; Luca Benini; Michele Magno
  4. Evaluating Human Pose Estimation Algorithms for Resource-Constrained Smart Eyewear DeviceHao Quan; Francesca Palermo; Simone Mentasti; Diana Trojaniello; Matteo Matteucci
