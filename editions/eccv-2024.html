Eyes of the Future

Integrating Computer Vision in Smart Eyewear (ICVSE)

The first workshop in the series established a dedicated forum for smart-eyewear computer vision, connecting algorithmic advances with computation, energy, privacy and usability constraints.

Workshop contributions

Accepted Papers

Twelve papers spanning eye tracking, event-based sensing, visual-inertial SLAM, efficient transformers, pose estimation and egocentric action recognition.

  1. Helios: An extremely low power event-based gesture recognition for always-on smart eyewear
  2. Towards Resource-aware Visual Inertial SLAM
  3. Ultra-Efficient On-Device Object Detection on AI-Integrated Smart Glasses With TinyissimoYOLO
  4. Evaluating Human Pose Estimation Algorithms for Resource-Constrained Smart Eyewear Device
  5. Selective Merging Schedule for Vision Transformers on Edge Devices
  6. Towards Real-Time Online Egocentric Action Recognition on Smart Eyewear
  7. Normalized Validity Scores for DNNs in Regression based Eye Feature Extraction and Real-Time Models for the Raspberry Pi
  8. Real-Time 2nd-order Gaze Metrics
  9. CondSeg: Ellipse Estimation of Pupil and Iris via Conditioned Segmentation
  10. Towards Unsupervised Eye-Region Segmentation for Eye Tracking
  11. Towards Low-power, High-frequency Gaze Direction Tracking with an Event-camera
  12. High-frequency near-eye ground truth for event-based eye tracking
