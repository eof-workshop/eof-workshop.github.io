Workshop contributions

Accepted Papers

Seven accepted papers covering efficient embedded vision, adaptive smart eyewear, eye tracking, tensor quantization, eye segmentation, neuromorphic sensing and active learning.

  1. A Comparative Study of Lightweight Vision Models on Embedded Accelerator DevicesGreta Corti; Christian Veronesi; Pietro Bartoli; Sara Pidò; Andrea Giudici; Francesca Palermo; Diana Trojaniello; Matteo Matteucci; Simone Mentasti
  2. Adaptive Tint Control for Smart Electrochromic EyewearSimon Weinberger; Jairo Cugliari
  3. Deep Neural Networks with an Adaptive Runtime for Eye Tracking on Smart GlassesWolfgang Fuhl
  4. Energy-efficient management of AI applications for Smart Eyewears with tensor quantizationAbednego Wamuhindo Kambale; Samin Shokrivahed; Giacomo Verticale; Francesca Palermo; Diana Trojaniello; Danilo Ardagna
  5. Maximizing Eye Segmentation Accuracy with YOLOArianna De Vecchi; Sabrina Azzi; Pietro Bartoli; Marco Paracchini; Diana Trojaniello; Federica Villa
  6. Neuromorphic eye tracking: a surveySimone Mentasti; Andrea Simpsi; Andrea Aspesi; Aaron Tognoli; Luca Merigo; Matteo Matteucci
  7. TActiLE: Tiny Active LEarning for wearable devicesMassimo Pavan; Claudio Galimberti; Manuel Roveri
