Overview

About EOF

Smart eyewear is an increasingly important real-world platform for computer vision, offering an always-available first-person view for accessibility, navigation, interaction, activity understanding, memory augmentation and augmented reality.

Deployable systems must jointly satisfy accuracy, latency, energy, heat, memory, privacy, robustness and form-factor constraints. Recent progress in egocentric vision, multimodal and foundation models, gaze estimation, efficient neural architectures and low-power sensing makes this a timely moment to connect algorithmic advances with wearable deployment.

EOF brings together researchers and practitioners from computer vision, machine learning, embedded AI, AR/XR, eye tracking, wearable sensing and human-computer interaction. Smart glasses are the primary application domain, while relevant work on eye tracking and egocentric perception across related devices is explicitly welcome.

Call for Papers

EOF focuses on computer vision and AI for smart glasses and deployable smart-eyewear systems. We also welcome relevant work developed using XR headsets, head-mounted cameras, wearable sensors, mobile devices and related visual platforms.

01

Efficient vision

Real-time, low-latency, compact and resource-aware vision models.

02

Egocentric perception

Action recognition, activity anticipation, scene understanding and 3D mapping.

03

Eye & gaze

Eye tracking, gaze estimation, near-eye sensing and gaze-aware interaction.

04

Multimodal wearables

Context awareness, personalization and vision-language methods.

05

Low-power sensing

Event-based, neuromorphic and low-power sensing and processing.

06

Responsible deployment

Privacy-preserving learning, robustness, safety, fairness and responsible deployment.

07

Datasets & benchmarks

Datasets, benchmarks and evaluation protocols for smart eyewear and related platforms.

08

Applications

Deployable applications for smart eyewear and related first-person visual devices.
